## Start Date for Deleting Cloud Recordings Postponed to March 1, 2027

Regarding Zoom accessed via UTokyo Account, the retention period for cloud recordings is currently unlimited; however, since June 2026, we have announced plans to change this setting to a uniform retention period of 3 years starting October 1, 2026. Under this change, cloud recordings were to be retained for up to 3 years from their creation date, and recordings older than 3 years were scheduled to be deleted sequentially.

However, because explanations regarding the scope of target files for deletion and backup procedures were not sufficiently clear, **we will postpone the start date for deleting cloud recordings to March 1, 2027**.

Further details, including how to verify files subject to deletion and how to back them up, will continue to be provided via the utelecon portal site.
