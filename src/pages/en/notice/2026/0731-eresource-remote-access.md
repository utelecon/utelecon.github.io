---
title: "Change to the Off-Campus Access Method for Electronic Resources (Effective August 10, 2026)"
---

The University of Tokyo Library System has announced that, starting Monday, August 10, 2026, the method for members of the University of Tokyo to access e-journals, e-books, and databases from off campus will change. EZproxy will be replaced by the single sign-on (SSO) service OpenAthens.

## Overview of the Change

- **Effective date**: Monday, August 10, 2026
- **What will change**: The method for accessing electronic resources from off campus will change from EZproxy to OpenAthens.
- **Authentication**: Your UTokyo Account will continue to be used.

Around the effective date, off-campus access to some e-journals, e-books, and databases may be temporarily unavailable. We appreciate your understanding.

## Details of the New Access Method

Detailed instructions for the new access method will be announced by the University of Tokyo Library System at a later date. For the latest information, please refer to "[Notice of Change to Off-Campus Access Method for Electronic Resources (Effective August 10, 2026)](https://www.lib.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/library/literacy/news/20260708-0)".

The descriptions of EZproxy on utelecon pages will be updated after the change.

## Contact

For questions about how to use electronic resources, please contact the University of Tokyo Library System through the [Ask Service](https://opac.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/opac/ask/?lang=1). For questions about your UTokyo Account, please contact the **[Technical Support Desk](/en/support/)**.
