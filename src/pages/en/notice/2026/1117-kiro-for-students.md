title: Free Access to Kiro for Students through the "Kiro for Students" Program
---

The University of Tokyo has been selected as a participating institution in the Kiro for Students Program. Currently enrolled students can use 1,000 Kiro credits per month for up to 12 months, free of charge (normally equivalent to USD 240 per year).

Kiro is an agentic development tool used for building software. For class projects, hackathons, startup MVPs and more, tell Kiro what you want to build, and it turns your idea into specs and working applications.

## Eligibility

Only currently enrolled students are eligible. Faculty and staff are not eligible. You must meet all four of the following requirements:

- Be 18 years of age or older
- Be currently enrolled at The University of Tokyo
- Have a Kiro account created with your University of Tokyo email address
- Be able to provide proof of enrollment upon request

Benefits are limited to one per person (you cannot reapply after the 12-month period ends). Benefits cannot be transferred or re-issued.

Please also refer to the [Kiro for Students Terms and Conditions](https://kiro.dev/students/terms-2026/).

## Benefits

- 1,000 credits per month for up to 12 months, free of charge.
- Access to models available in paid plans, on IDE / CLI / Web / Crew.
- No credit card required.
- Unused credits do not roll over to the next month, and additional credits cannot be purchased.
- After 12 months, your account automatically moves to the free plan.

### Use in Classes and Research

**Whether generative AI may be used in a class is decided by the instructor, depending on the nature of the class.** Please **follow the instructions of your department, program, and course instructors, and do not use generative AI where it is not permitted.** If you are asked to describe how you used AI when submitting an assignment, please follow those instructions.

Journals and conferences may also prohibit the use of generative AI or impose conditions such as disclosure. Please check their policies carefully before submitting.

### Responsibility for Generated Output

Code and documents generated by Kiro may contain errors, behavior that differs from your intent, or security vulnerabilities. Generated code may also resemble existing code, which can raise license or copyright issues. **You are responsible for the output you use.** Please understand and review generated code before using it.

### Handling of the Information You Enter

Kiro is not a service contracted by the University of Tokyo as an organization; each user agrees to the AWS terms individually. Content you enter in Kiro is sent to the AWS cloud, where the AI models run.

**By default, your content (such as questions and code) may be used by AWS for service improvement.** To opt out, follow these steps:

1. Open Settings in Kiro.
2. Go to the "User" tab → "Application" → "Telemetry and Content."
3. Uncheck the following two boxes:
   - Usage Analytics And Performance Metrics
   - Content Collection for Service Improvement

Even after opting out, your content is still sent to AWS. Do not enter information that requires careful handling, such as exam or assignment questions, non-public code or data provided by your laboratory or research partners, unpublished research results, personal information, or any other information that should not be known outside the people concerned. If you are unsure, consult the person responsible for that information (such as your supervisor or research partner).

## Getting Started

Please use your **[ECCS Cloud Email](/en/google/) address (`@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp`)** for student verification.

1. Sign up or sign in to Kiro (IDE / CLI / Web / Crew).
2. Open Settings, go to "Students," select The University of Tokyo, and click "verify."
3. On the SheerID screen, enter your name, date of birth, and university email address (`@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp`). Additional documents may be requested.
4. Once verified, your plan name changes to "Kiro Students" and you can start using it immediately.

Student verification can be done only once. Please make sure your information is correct. For the sign-up page and detailed instructions, please refer to the official Kiro pages:

- [Kiro for Students](https://kiro.dev/students/)
- [Detailed instructions](https://kiro.dev/students/#instructions)
