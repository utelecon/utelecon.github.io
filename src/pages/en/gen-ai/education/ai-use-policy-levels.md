## Classification of AI Use Scenarios

| Scene | Name                     | Description                                                                                      |
|-------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| S1    | questions/research       | ask questions, look up background information, etc.                                              |
| S2    | discussion/idea-bouncing | discuss with AI, bounce ideas, ask possible topics to write about, etc.                          |
| S3    | feedback                 | ask AI to review your answers or reports to submit                                               |
| S4    | drafting                 | ask AI to draft your submission you then build on and finalize                                   |
| S5    | creative use             | creative usages beyond using AI for help (critically review AI output, experiment with AI, etc.) |

## Policy Labels to Display to Students 

* Instructors should indicate which one applies to each assignment
