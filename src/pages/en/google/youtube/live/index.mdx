This page explains how to use YouTube Live with an ECCS Cloud Mail account, focusing specifically on enabling and broadcasting live streams. Please refer to this page when live streaming is required for university events.

If you want to stream a Zoom meeting or webinar to YouTube, please refer to "[Stream Zoom Meetings or Webinars on YouTube](./streaming/zoom/)". Also, for instructions on configuring visibility settings (such as restricting access exclusively to University of Tokyo members), refer to "[Set YouTube Video Visibility](../sharing/)".

**When live streaming, pay close attention to whether the visibility is set to "Public" or "Unlisted". If set to "Public", anyone can view the stream on YouTube. If set to "Unlisted", only people with the unique URL generated for that specific stream can view it. If you do not wish to broadcast to the general public, please use "Unlisted".**

## Precautions for Use

* The "Unlisted URL" for a live stream changes every time you stream. Please share the link with the intended viewers once the stream has started.
* To prepare for unforeseen circumstances, we recommend enabling live streaming on multiple accounts in advance.
* Make sure to double-check the "Public" or "Unlisted" settings at the start of your live stream to avoid errors.
* We recommend conducting a test stream 1 to 2 weeks prior to the actual event, using the exact same location, equipment, and environment.
* Viewers may experience playback issues depending on their individual network or device environment. Additionally, if an outage occurs within YouTube's streaming system, the service may become unavailable.

## Live Stream Setup

The verification environment used for this page is as follows:

* Operating system：macOS 26.6.2 (25G83)
* Web browser：Google Chrome 153.0.8010.37 (Official Build) (arm64)

### How to Enable Live Streaming
{:#enable-live}

It takes about 24 hours from creating a YouTube channel until you can start live streaming, so please make sure to allow enough time.

You must create a YouTube channel using your ECCS Cloud Mail account in advance. Please refer to "[Create a Channel](../##create-channel)" for the channel creation procedure. Once the channel is created, enable live streaming by following the steps below:

1. Open the home page and click the "+ Create" button in the upper right corner of the screen.
    ![](./create-channel.drawio.png){:.small.border}
2. Select "Go live".
    ![](./create-live-start.drawio.png){:.small.border}
3. When the "Request access to streaming" screen appears, click the "Request" button.
    ![](./request-live-streaming.png){:.small}
4. Perform [Account Verification](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171664).
    1. Click the "Verify" button.
        ![](./verify-account-0.drawio.png){:.small}
    1. Check the icon on the right side of the screen to make sure the open page belongs to the account you want to use for live streaming.
    1. Select a verification method. You can choose either an automated voice message or a text message (SMS).
        ![](./verify-account-1.drawio.png){:.small.border}
    1. Confirm that the selected country matches the phone number you are entering, then enter your phone number.
        ![](./verify-account-2.drawio.png){:.small.border}
    1. Enter the 6-digit code you received.
        ![](./verify-account-3.drawio.png){:.medium.border}
    1. Once the account verification is successful, return to the original page.
        ![](./verify-account-4.drawio.png){:.small.border}
    5. Wait approximately 24 hours for live streaming to be activated.
    ![](./live-wait.png){:.small}

### Starting a Live Stream
{:#start-live}

Please do this after completing [How to Enable Live Streaming](#enable-live).

1. Open the home page, click the "+ Create" button in the upper right corner of the screen, and select "Go live".
    ![](./start-live.drawio.png){:.small}
1. Click "Webcam".
    ![](./live-studio.drawio.png){:.small}
1. Enter the live stream details.
    * Stream Title
      ![](./live-name.drawio.png){:.small}
    * Audience and Age Restriction
    * Set audience and age restriction as necessary. For "Is this video made for kids?", normally select "No, it's not made for kids".
        ![](./live-age.drawio.png){:.small}
1. Select whether to enable or disable live chat.
    * Under "Customization", there is an option to enable or disable live chat from viewers.
    * It is enabled by default, so uncheck the box to disable it if necessary.
    ![](./live-chat.drawio.png){:.small}
1. Select the visibility setting.
    * Select the appropriate setting from "Public", "Unlisted", or "Private".
    * Please note that setting it to "Public" makes the stream visible to anyone on YouTube.
    * Setting it to "Unlisted" allows anyone with the live stream URL to watch it.

    \* The following explanations assume that "Unlisted" is selected.
    ![](./live-public.drawio.png){:.small}
1. Set up your camera and microphone, then check the stream preview.
    * Select your camera under "Video" and your microphone under "Audio".
        * You can choose USB cameras and microphones in addition to built-in ones.
    * Check the camera preview, camera and microphone settings, and visibility setting ("Privacy"), then click "Go Live".
    ![](./live-start.drawio.png){:.small}
   1. The live stream will begin.
    * The live video feed will be displayed.
    ![](./live.drawio.png){:.small}
    * Click the arrow button in the upper right corner of the screen to display the URL for "Unlisted" access.
        ![](./live-share.drawio.png){:.small}
        * Copy the URL and share it with intended viewers via email or other means.
        ![](./live-share-url.drawio.png){:.small}
   1. To end the stream, click "End Stream" in the upper right corner of the screen.
    ![](./live-end.drawio.png){:.small}
