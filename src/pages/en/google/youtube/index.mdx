**YouTube** is a video streaming service provided by Google in which you can upload and release a video, or offer a livestreaming of an event. 

If you are one of students, faculty and staff of the University of Tokyo can use YouTube with your [ECCS Cloud Email account](/en/google). With your ECCS Cloud Email account, you can share a video of a lecture, a livestreamed ceremony and any other content **only with students, faculty and staff of the University of Tokyo.**

This page describes [how to upload a video and offer a livestreaming](#for-creators) and [how to watch contents shared only with students, faculty and staff of the University of Tokyo](#watching). If you need to know basic procedures to use Youtube, please visit [Google's official help page](https://support.google.com/youtube?hl=en&sjid=14250656160075261256-NC#topic=9257498). 

## To Upload a Video and Offer a Livestreaming
{:#for-creators}

Follow the procedures below to upload a video and offer a livestreaming on YouTube with your ECCS Cloud Email account. Please [create your channel](#create-channel) first and [upload a video](#upload), [complete video privacy settings](#sharing) or offer a livestreaming(#live) according your purpose. 

### About Your Channel and Account
{:#channel}

To upload a video or offer a livestreaming on YouTube, you need **your channel** linked to your account. 

There are two kinds of YouTube channels: **created with a personal account** and **created with a brand account.** Also, **on a channel created by a personal account, you can share contents only with students, faculty and staff of the University of Tokyo.** **If you upload a video or offer a livestreaming only for students, faculty and staff of the University of Tokyo**, you need to **use a channel created with your personal ECCS Cloud Email Account (instead of the brand account of the University of Tokyo). On the other hand, on a channel created with a brand account, you can manage the account with other members and create plural channels with one account.

### To Create a Channel
{:#create-channel}

Before uploading a video or offering a livestreaming, you need to create your channel with your ECCS Cloud Email account. Follow the procedures below to log in YouTube with your ECCS Cloud Email account and create a channel with your personal account. **To create a channel with a brand account, please visit "[Create a YouTube channel](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861?hl=en&sjid=12429287744673113938-NC)">"Create a channel with a business or other name."** 

1. Log in YouTube with your ECCS Cloud Email account.
2. Click "Create" on the upper right of the screen. 
3. Click "Upload video" or "Go Live."
4. Enter the profile of your channel. 
5. Click "Create channel."

## To Watch a Video Only Shared with Students, Faculty and Staff of the University of Tokyo
{:#watching}

To watch a video only shared with students, faculty and staff of the University of Tokyo, **you need to log in YouTube with your ECCS Cloud Email account.**

Each of the error messages below will be displayed when you try to watch such a video without logging in or with another account. 

- ["**Private video: Sign in if you've been granted access to this video**"（The "Log in" banner **IS** in the center of the display）](#watching-private)
- ["**Video unavailable: This video is private**"（The "Log in" banner is **NOT** on the display）](#watching-cannot-play)

Please follow the procedures below to watch the video with your ECCS Cloud Email account in each case.

### When The Video Is Displayed as "Private"
{:#watching-private}

When you try to watch a video only shared with the students and faculties without logging in, the video will be displayed as "Private."


In this case, please follow the procedures below to log in and watch the video. 

1. Click the "Log in" banner in the center of the display. 
    - If there is no "Log in" banner on the display, please check "["When The Video Is Displayed as 'Video unavailable'"](#watching-cannot-play)."
2. Enter your ECCS Cloud Email address (one setup with the username of your choice, or your 10-digit Common ID + `@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp`) on Google's login screen. 
3. Sign in your [UTokyo Account](/utokyo_account/) to watch the video. 

### When The Video Is Displayed as "Video unavailable"
{:#watching-cannot-play}


This error message will be displayed in the cases below. 

- You have logged in with an account **other than** your ECCS Cloud Email account.．
- You use another account to watch YouTube although you have logged in with your ECCS Cloud Email account. 

In these cases, please follow the procedures below to log in YouTube with your ECCS Cloud Email account. 

1. Visit [the top page of YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) and click the icon of your account on the upper right of the screen (if you have not set up an image as your icon, part of your name or your initial may be displayed as your icon).
2. Click "Switch account."
3. Select your ECCS Cloud Email account (the account with the domain `@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp`) if it is on the displayed list of your accounts used to log in. 
    - If your ECCS Cloud Email account is not on the list, please select "Add account," log in with your ECCS Cloud Email account described as "[When The Video Is Displayed as 'Video unavailable']" and access the video again. 

4. Access the video again.
